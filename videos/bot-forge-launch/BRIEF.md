## Intent

This video quote-reposts the author's own Sep 17 post — "Grok has a bot that builds bots. I built
the same thing for @NousResearch Hermes Agent, and I'm open-sourcing it"
(https://x.com/BkashJosi/status/2100528442752323623). That post made a claim; this video closes
the loop: it is now reviewed and listed in the official Hermes plugin catalog, installable by
anyone with one command.

So the video is **proof, not a pitch** — a show-it-as-is tour of the real catalog page: the
listing with its Community tier, version and star count, the install command, the tools it adds.
Feature the page's own captured screens as the video's assets; do not rebuild the page.

Tone: confident, dry, developer-facing. No hype adjectives, no stock-video gloss. It should
look like the product it is selling — dark, precise, a little playful because the product's
mark is a friendly bot.
